About Ramakrishna Mission Ashrama, Jhargram

Ramakrishna Mission Ashrama, Jhargram, a branch centre of the Ramakrishna Mission, Belur Math came into existence on 20th August 2017 on five acres of land obtained on lease from the Government of West Bengal. Srimat Swami Smarananandaji Maharaj, the 16th President of Ramakrishna Math and Ramakrishna Mission, Belur Math, inaugurated the newly constructed Assembly cum Prayer Hall in the Ashrama campus on 7th of December 2019.

The following activities/projects are carried out by the Ashrama at present:

Educational

  • Ramakrishna Mission Vidyamandira (EMRS), Jhargram: Residential School for Tribal students (460 students).
  • 6 Free Coaching Centers: 320 students receive free coaching, uniforms, and light tiffin.
  • Scholarships: In 2023-24, scholarships worth 13.41 lakhs were awarded to 152 deserving students.

Welfare

  • Distribution of clothes, relief assistance, and financial aid to underprivileged families.
  • Disaster relief and winter jacket/blanket distribution.
  • Monthly motivational programs at Jhargram Correctional Home.
  • Bi-monthly free homeopathic health camp by Dr. Ashoke Pradhan.
  • Annual blood donation drive with over 100 donors.
  • Swami Vivekananda Tree Plantation (16,000+ trees planted).
  • Monthly pension of Rs. 1,200 to 6 widows.

Youth

  • ‘Arise, Awake’: Value education across 32 institutions covering 8,000 students and 400 teachers.
  • Annual district football tournament, yoga, and cultural competitions.
  • Vivekananda study circles at 12 centers.
  • National Youth Day celebrations and Sunday moral classes.

Spiritual

  • Birth anniversary celebrations of Sri Ramakrishna, Sri Sarada Devi, and Swami Vivekananda.
  • Daily evening prayer, religious discourses, and Ekadashi Ramnam Sankirtan.
  • Spiritual retreats and distribution of Vedanta literature.
